Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch New

Kneel on one knee, push hips forward gently until you feel a stretch at the front of the hip. Keep your back upright. Both sides · 45 sec hold each.

Lying Quad Stretch New

Lie face down. Bend one knee and gently pull your foot toward your glute with your hand. Feel the stretch along the front of the thigh. Both sides · 30 sec hold each.

Dead Bug New

Lie on your back, arms pointing up, knees bent at 90 degrees. Slowly lower one arm overhead while extending the opposite leg, keeping your lower back pressed flat. Return and alternate. 2 sets of 8 reps each side.
